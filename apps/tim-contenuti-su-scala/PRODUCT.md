## Users

## Product Purpose

An Italian-first, immersive Experience Design Factory perspective that opens a working table on industrializing campaign content production: video, spots, social posts and images, with incremental optimization. Success is agreement to map one real campaign workflow and define a measurable pilot, not a product demo or a purchase decision.

## Positioning

The experience follows one campaign brief through a governed content-supply loop: a shared idea becomes approved channel variations; performance evidence informs the next iteration. The loop is a proposal to test with TIM, not a claim about its current process. The experience is not a catalog or a promise that one application replaces broadcast production.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The experience presents an illustrative end-to-end campaign workflow and a proposed discovery route. It has no live software demo.
- Show the full loop across production volume, cycle time/cost, and campaign effectiveness; establish TIM's baselines before making a business case.
- Do not claim current workflow bottlenecks, savings, performance lift, AI adoption in campaign production, or the completeness of TIM's AI Data Lab without confirmation.
- Do not present GenStudio for Performance Marketing as a broadcast-spot production system. Confirm how it would connect to TIM's existing creative, agency, asset-management, legal-approval and media-measurement processes.
- All client-facing copy is institutional, not addressed to an individual. Product names and public claims require source checks.

## Product Principles

1. Start from one real campaign brief, not from a product.
2. Treat brand, legal and human creative review as part of the production system.
3. Distinguish broadcast masters from digital adaptations; connect them only where TIM's workflow supports it.
4. Use measured outcomes to decide what to scale, refresh or stop.
5. Label hypotheses and illustrative workflows; ask TIM to validate them.
