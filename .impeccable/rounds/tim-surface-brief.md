# Una campagna, molti formati

Prospettiva in italiano, prima della discovery, per convincere Marketing e campaign leadership ad aprire un tavolo e definire un pilot misurabile. Il primo interlocutore è lo sponsor social/data; coinvolgere l'owner Marketing/Brand e produzione quando sarà confermato. La experience segue le campagne ATL e BTL del brand TIM, non i servizi TIM Enterprise per i propri clienti.

The reader should understand how one campaign brief could become governed video, spot, post and image variants, and how evidence could inform a next iteration. The public 2025 campaign is proof of channel variety, not proof of TIM's current workflow or its performance. No cost, time, volume, lift, adoption or process claim without a baseline. Distinguish broadcast production from digital activation; label the proposed loop as a hypothesis.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A campaign is a section through one idea: the core remains visible as the work opens into formats and returns with evidence. Refuse the SaaS feature tour and the generic asset gallery.

**OWN-WORLD:** A daylight architectural section becomes a production-system drawing, not a literal building. White carries the page; verified TIM blue structures the cut and its paths; TIM red marks a review or decision. Technical rules, sectional layers and a single path unify navigation, content and state. No imported beige/gold palette.

**STORY:** Start from a public campaign example and its documented channel range; separate what is known from what must be mapped with TIM. Move through the proposed flow, the working hypotheses and a route to a pilot. The memorable moment is one line passing through the core idea, branching into recognizable formats, then returning as a question about measured results.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** At 1920×1080 the campaign-section drawing owns the centre and most of the width. A left-aligned headline names the proposal; the cut reads left-to-right as brief → core creative → ATL/BTL variants → evidence, with a visible return path. A short source note anchors the public example; one clear action begins the journey. The section labels remain legible at mobile width.

**FORM:** Assigned direction, fourth in the seven grounded candidates; seed key `39b28f0c`. Code-first. Keep TIM's actual campaign language, real formats and explicit uncertainty; no fabricated metrics, interfaces or process evidence.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
